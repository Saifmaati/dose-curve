# DoseCurve audit — 2026-09-27

Scope: regression, correctness, security and test-coverage audit of `main` at `dfa81e9`, before the Custom Regimen Timeline. Branch: `audit-and-test-hardening`. Only defects that could be reproduced were fixed; no features were added.

**Method**
- Ran `node --test tests/` before and after every change: 41 passing at the start, 47 passing at the end.
- Probed `pk-engine.js` in Node with malformed, hostile and boundary inputs.
- Checked the page in a browser at 1280 px and 375 px: DOM, console, accessibility tree, and render timing.

## Architecture observations

The scenario lifecycle is sound and has a single path for each stage:

1. **Defaults:** `PK.DEFAULTS` and `PK.VIEW_DEFAULTS`.
2. **UI input:** `state`, then `changed(keys)`. In Compare mode, `PK.cmpApply` updates the edited side.
3. **Normalization:** `decodeScenario` clamps every value, and `normalizeScenario` enforces the rules that span two fields.
4. **Simulation:** everything goes through `doseEvents(p)`, then `conc(p,t)` by superposition.
5. **Metrics:** `derived`, `windowStats`, `ssProfile` and `compareRows`.
6. **Output:** the chart (`render`), the panels (`renderPanels`), and CSV/PNG export.
7. **Share link:** `encodeLink`, then `decodeLink` and `applyLink`.

`pk-engine.js` has no DOM access, and every Compare operation (apply, copy, swap, lock, reset) is a pure function that returns a new object. That's the right base for the timeline. `doseEvents(p)` is the one place that turns settings into doses, so simulation, window metrics and CSV export will follow custom events automatically once it understands them.

## Verified findings and fixes

| # | Finding | How it showed up | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A missed dose outside the regimen was kept | A link with `missed:15` on a 6-dose regimen showed "Missed dose: dose 15" while no dose was missed. The same happened to B when "Vary only: missed dose" was on and A's dose count shrank. | New `normalizeScenario` clears it. It's applied in `decodeScenario`, `cmpApply`, `cmpSetLock` and `loadScenario` |
| 2 | A hand-edited link could keep a broken "Vary only" lock | `lk=D` survived even though A and B also differed in half-life | New `lockHolds` check; `decodeLink` drops a lock the scenarios don't honour |
| 3 | Names kept text-direction override characters | U+202E and similar could reverse how a shared name reads | Removed along with other control characters, and names are still limited to 40 characters |
| 4 | The Simulator's note showed a meaningless peak-to-trough ratio | Half-life 0.5 h dosed every 24 h gave "peak-to-trough 172,702,659,389×" | Uses the steady-state panel's 1% rule and says the drug clears almost completely between doses |
| 5 | Lesson scroll ignored reduced-motion | `scrollIntoView({behavior:"smooth"})` ran even when the user asks for reduced motion | Scrolls instantly in that case |
| 6 | MEC/MTC inputs had no accessible name | Their `<label>`s weren't linked to the inputs | Added `for="mec"` and `for="mtc"` |

New tests, 6 added (47 total):
- a missed dose outside the regimen is cleared
- lock plus a shrinking dose count can't strand a missed dose
- hand-edited lock links are rejected
- names lose control and direction characters
- malformed, truncated and 200 KB links degrade safely
- the worst-case steady state stays within 0.5%

One existing test changed: its fixture locked "dose" while A and B also differed in interval. The app can't produce that, and fix 2 now rejects it.

## Checked and found sound

- **Input validation:** Infinity, NaN, zero intervals and half-lives, `__proto__`/`constructor` keys and broken percent-encoding are all rejected or clamped. A 200 KB name decodes in about 1 ms and is cut to 40 characters.
- **Injection safety:** every piece of text from a link or the user that reaches `innerHTML` (scenario names, baseline label) goes through `esc()`. Everything else is set with `textContent`, or is a number the engine has already validated. The page makes no network requests beyond Google Fonts, and links carry no personal data.
- **Event listeners:** each is attached once at startup, or to a slider that's rebuilt from scratch. Nothing piles up across renders or mode switches.
- **Scenario independence:** Compare operations never modify their input, and there are tests for reset, copy, swap and lock.
- **Division by zero:** every denominator has a positive lower bound (t½ ≥ 0.5 h, organ function ≥ 25%, V ≥ 5 L, weight ≥ 40 kg, τ ≥ 2 h, infusion ≥ 0.25 h).
- **Render time, measured:**
  - typical Simulator update: about 2 ms
  - heaviest Compare case (both sides 20 doses, τ 2 h, t½ 24 h at 25% organ function, 168 h window): about 9 ms median, 18 ms maximum
- **Unchanged outputs:** the defaults are still Cmax 9.3, AUC 74.2 and 48% in window, and the lesson values match `main`.

## Non-blocking concerns

- Steady state is found by simulating up to 400 doses. In the slowest-clearing case that's 99.7% accurate (tested at ≥ 99.5%). A closed-form steady state would remove the cap.
- MEC can be set above MTC. Nothing breaks, but "time in window" then can't be above zero. A UI warning would help.
- Values from a link that fall between slider steps (such as a 25 h window) are used exactly. The label shows the real value, but the slider thumb snaps to the nearest step.
- The tabs use `role="tab"` without `aria-controls` or arrow-key support. The scenario cards are clickable `div`s that can't be focused by keyboard, though the "Editing" switch provides the same action with buttons.
- CSV export always covers the full time window, even when the chart is zoomed to the final interval. That's deliberate, but it isn't stated in the UI.
- The PNG export renders text in fallback fonts, because the web fonts aren't embedded in the image.
- "Visualize on curve" in Practice keeps any existing baseline, so it can compare against an unrelated scenario.
- Drug presets only set the values they specify. Everything else keeps its previous value, which is the existing design.
- Links carry a version number (`v`) but nothing acts on it; newer versions are read best-effort.
- GitHub still shows Pages run #4 as "queued". It can't be cancelled from the UI and has no effect; later deploys succeeded.

### Follow-up (2026-09-28)

- **Steady state:** now exact. `ssConc` sums every earlier dose as geometric series, so the 400-dose cap is gone.
- **MEC ≥ MTC:** the Therapeutic Window section shows a warning and marks both fields invalid.
- **Tabs:** each tab names its panel (`aria-controls`/`tabpanel`), the tab list is one tab stop, and arrow keys, Home and End move between tabs. The scenario cards are unchanged: focusing a card's name field already switches sides, and the Editing switch offers the same action as buttons.
- **CSV export:** the button's tooltip says it covers the full window, and a notice says so when the chart is zoomed to the final interval.
- **"Visualize on curve":** clears the baseline.
- **Link versions:** a link from a newer version is flagged, and the page says some settings may not have come through.
- **Steady state scope:** `ssProfile` and `ssConc` return `null` unless the regimen is regular repeated dosing, so the engine enforces it and not only the page.
- **Overlapping infusions:** `infusionOverlap` finds infusions running at once, and the page says so (in Compare, per scenario) when the infusion time exceeds the interval or custom infusions overlap.
- Still open: slider thumbs snapping for off-step link values, fonts in the PNG export, and the stuck Pages run.

## Recommendations for the Custom Regimen Timeline data model

1. **Events as scenario data.** Add `regimen: "basic" | "custom"` and `events: [{t, mg, missed}]` to the scenario. Use absolute milligrams per event, so a loading dose is simply a larger event. Keep one route per scenario in V1, with infusions using the scenario's `tinf`.
2. **Extend `doseEvents(p)` first.** In custom mode it should return the given events, sorted. Simulation, window metrics and CSV export then work without further changes. Define what happens when two events share a time: they should add together.
3. **Code that assumes a regular regimen** needs a custom-mode path, or should return `null` so the UI shows "—":
   - Engine: `derived` (final interval `(n−1)τ…nτ`, `Rac`, `t90`, `fSS`), `ssProfile` (rows per τ, steady-state extrapolation), `compareRows` (trough, accumulation, swing and time-to-steady-state rows), `relevant`, `missedOf`.
   - Page: `finalInterval`/`viewRange`, the dose-marker loop (`i<nDoses`, `i*tau`), `renderNote`, the steady-state panel, the regimen sentences in `explain`, `updateRegime`, `scenarioSummary`, the auto-extending time window in the repeated-dosing toggle, and the τ, n and missed-dose sliders.
4. **Arrays break today's shallow copies.** `newComparison`, `cmpCopy`, `cmpSwap`, `cmpApply` and the page's `snapshot` use `Object.assign`, which would share one `events` array between A and B. Either deep-copy `events` in those functions, or treat events as immutable and replace the whole array on every edit. Add a test that mutates A's events and checks B is untouched.
5. **Arrays break today's equality checks.** `encodeScenario`, `paramDiffs` and `lockHolds` compare with `===`, so arrays need a structural comparison. Decide what "Vary only" means for events: lock the whole regimen, or disable the lock while in custom mode.
6. **Link format v2.** Encode events compactly, for example `ev:0@500;12@250;24@250m` (`m` = missed). Keep decoding v1 links as basic regimens. Cap the number of events (for example 40), clamp `t` to 0–168 h and the amount to the dose range, sort on decode, and put all of that in `normalizeScenario`.
7. **Performance.** Render cost grows with events × 600 samples × curves. The worst case is 9–18 ms today, so at 40 events consider caching each scenario's samples when only view settings change.
8. **Tests to add with the timeline:** irregular timing, a delayed dose, several events at one time, loading and missed events, invalid times and amounts, events surviving a link round trip and a CSV export, and A/B isolation under copy, swap, reset and lock.
